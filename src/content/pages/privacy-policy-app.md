_Last updated November 1, 2026_

This policy covers the Gretio app for iOS and Android, made by Surreal Development LLC ("we", "us"). Questions and requests: use our [contact form](https://surrealdev.com/contact/). Our website has [its own policy](/privacy-policy/).

## 1. Vehicle Data Stays on Your Device

VINs, scan results, trouble codes, recordings, and your dashboards are stored on your device. We don't receive them. The exception is if you email us app logs for support, which can include VINs and adapter names.

## 2. What We Collect

**Account.** If you sign in: your email, display name, and account identifier from Apple, Google, or email sign-in. Your password stays with that provider.

**Purchases.** Handled by the App Store or Google Play; we never see payment details. RevenueCat manages subscriptions for us and receives your account identifier and purchase history.

**Crash reports.** When the app crashes or hits an error, we receive technical details about the app, device, and what happened, linked to your account identifier if you're signed in. Network addresses, VINs, and device names are removed first.

**Usage data.** Which features are used, device and app information, your IP address, and an approximate location (country or region) derived from it.

Gretio shows no ads, uses no advertising identifiers, and doesn't request your location. Bluetooth and nearby-device access is used only to reach your vehicle adapter.

## 3. How We Use It

To provide your account and purchases; to find and fix problems and keep the service secure; to understand which features are used so we can improve them; and to meet legal obligations. Under GDPR these rest on contract, legitimate interest, and legal obligation respectively. You can use Gretio without an account; one is needed only for purchases.

## 4. Who We Share It With

Only service providers working for us: Google Firebase (sign-in, crash reports, usage data), Apple and Google (sign-in, purchases), and RevenueCat (subscriptions). We may also disclose data when the law requires it, to protect our users or our rights, or to a successor if the business is sold, who would be bound by this policy. We don't sell personal data or share it for targeted advertising.

We and our providers process data in the United States. Transfers from the EEA, UK, or Switzerland rely on safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 5. Retention and Deletion

- Account and purchase records: while your account exists. Deleting your account in the app removes it and the data stored with it on our servers; purchase records may remain with the app stores and RevenueCat as long as billing and law require.
- Crash reports: up to 90 days. Usage data tied to you: up to 2 months.

We use access controls, encryption in transit, and our providers' security measures. No system is completely secure.

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you can access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, object to or restrict its use, or withdraw consent. Sign out or delete your account in the app; for anything else use our [contact form](https://surrealdev.com/contact/). In the EEA, UK, or Switzerland you can also complain to your data protection authority.

## 7. Children

Gretio isn't for children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you think a child has given us data, [contact us](https://surrealdev.com/contact/) and we'll delete it.

## 8. Changes

We'll update the date above when this policy changes and tell you in the app about significant ones.
